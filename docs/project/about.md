## Why it exists

Heatmaps and session replay usually come from a hosted service: a third party script on every page, an account, and your visitors' behaviour stored on somebody else's servers. For many Django sites that is more than the job needs. Sightline does the useful part, where people click, how far they scroll, where they get stuck, inside the project you already run, with the database you already have.
